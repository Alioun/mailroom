# Personal-use readiness

Mailroom is a development prototype. Its local and synthetic workflows are implemented in bounded slices. It is not accepted for daily use with real accounts. Compilation, parser tests and server-rendered controls do not establish installed or provider behavior.

The personal target is this Mac, mailbox.org delivery rules, and the agreed Gmail forwarding route. Gmail's backlog stays in Gmail. Other platforms, general Gmail filter deployment and self-hosted multi-user operation belong to the later public product.

## Gates before real daily use

1. Complete the remaining personal features and pass applicable credential-free checks. Known loopback-listener and browser-launch restrictions are blocked checks, not passes. Do not remove an execution hold to compensate for missing evidence.
2. Verify the built interface through mounted interaction, including English/German controls, narrow layouts, paging, cancellation and recovery. Server rendering verifies markup and contracts only.
3. Verify the emitted executable with an independent Sieve engine, then exercise connected fixture transactions, process replacement and the whole personal workflow in an enforced environment that supports those fixtures.
4. Obtain exact-version approval for secret-handling code after credential-free behavior tests and its separate secret-flow review. The owner runs `scripts/setup.sh` and its `recovery` subcommand from the approved commit. Approval of an earlier commit does not transfer to a changed checkout.
5. Record owner-run read-only provider evidence and privately approve discovered roles, addresses, policy, invoice senders and accounting targets. Ownership confirmation does not assign a role or authorize forwarding.
6. After the complete personal build, obtain the owner's explicit authorization for live write validation. Validate coexistence, forwarding/archive settings, filing, flags, sends, unsubscribe and accounting intake before daily use. Only the owner decides when to resume broader public-product work.

These gates do not ask an agent to access credentials or operate an owner's session. Host provisioning, secret entry and provider operations remain owner steps in the approved wizard.

## Standing limits

Production provider writes remain denied independently of confirmation. Read-only IMAP evidence does not imply Sieve support, trusted delivery-envelope evidence or passing DKIM coverage. Unknown and mixed recipient roles retain Inbox without accounting copies or read-state changes. A separately verified Gmail ingress route can still add its route keyword under that hold.

The core sender lists require actual maintainer review; synthetic rows cannot complete them. Takeout import, broader agent integration and remaining provider reconciliation are unfinished. Platform work beyond this Mac is deferred. No synthetic receipt, local download, native approval or restored bundle grants provider-write authority.
